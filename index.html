📅 Tournament Schedule

Day 1

🔥 Free Fire Qualifier

Initial qualification rounds for Free Fire teams. Top teams advance to eliminator round.

Day 2

🎮 BGMI Qualifier

Initial qualification rounds for BGMI teams. Top teams advance to eliminator round.

Day 3

⚔️ Eliminator Round

Eliminator rounds for both Free Fire and BGMI. Teams compete to secure semi-final spots.

Day 4

🏆 Semi-Finals

Intense semi-final matches for both games. Only the best teams make it to the finals.

Day 5

👑 Final Match

The ultimate showdown! Champions will be crowned for both Free Fire and BGMI.
